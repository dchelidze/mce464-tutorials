MCE 464 Vibrations · Fall 2026

Geometric Tutorials

Interactive companions to the lecture slides. Each page turns one algebraic result into a picture you can move, with all parameters in a panel on the left. Numbered in lecture order.

Tutorial 1 · Lectures 2, 4, 10

Energy Landscape and Linearization

Potential wells, equilibria, tangent stiffness, phase portraits, and when the osculating parabola stops being a good model.
Tutorial 2 · Lectures 5–6, 10, 19

Phase Portraits and the Trace–Determinant Plane

Drag a point through the trace–determinant plane and watch a field of orbits flow into saddles, nodes, spirals and centers.
Tutorial 3 · Lectures 5–6

Roots in the s-Plane

Characteristic roots, the circle of radius \(\omega_n\), damping as an angle, with the mass moving alongside.
Tutorial 4 · Lectures 11–12

Phasors and the Forced Response

The force polygon, rotating phasors, magnitude and phase curves, and the mobility circle with its half-power points.
Tutorial 5 · Lectures 13–14

One Curve, Many Machines

Base excitation as a vector triangle with an animated suspension; transmissibility, seismometer, accelerometer and unbalance.
Tutorial 6 · Lecture 15

Dissipation Loops and the Nonlinear FRF

Hysteresis loops and loss per cycle for four damping models, then the bent, jumping frequency response and sweep direction.
Tutorial 7 · Lectures 16–17

Convolution as Superposition

The flipped kernel sweeping across the load, causality, step response and the time-step error of the discrete sum.
Tutorial 8 · Lecture 18

The System as a Harmonic Filter

The load spectrum laid over the frequency response: resonant harmonics, mean load, Gibbs overshoot and truncation.
Tutorial 9 · Lectures 20–21

Modes as Directions

Inertia and stiffness ellipses, the Rayleigh quotient, the singular dynamic-stiffness matrix, orthogonality and avoided crossings.
Tutorial 10 · Lectures 21–22

Energy Surfaces and the Mass-Weighted Transform

Rotating 3D kinetic and potential bowls morphing through \(\vb{M}^{1/2}\vb{q}\) into modal coordinates.
Tutorial 11 · Lecture 22

Modal Coordinates

Change of axes that decouples the equations, modal truncation with static correction, and nonproportional damping.
Tutorial 12 · Lecture 23

The Tuned Absorber

Antiresonance, the two peaks, the fixed points of the damped absorber, and the Den Hartog optimum.
Tutorial 13 · Lecture 24

Beam Modes and Modal Projection

End conditions and the frequency equation for \(\beta L\), animated mode shapes, orthogonality as cancelling areas, and generalized forces with modal truncation checked against the exact static solution.
Tutorial 14 · Lectures 8–9

Virtual Displacements and Equivalent Properties

Lever, rolling cylinder, pulley and gear pair: drag a virtual displacement, watch geometry ratios enter inertia and stiffness as squares, and check that \(\omega_n\) does not depend on the coordinate.

Pages are self-contained (KaTeX is bundled in assets/katex), so they open offline from this folder. Equations are typeset in LaTeX; the labels drawn inside the plots are plain text.

Colours follow the NLD figure style: MATLAB default ColorOrder with dark green and plum substitutions for colour-vision safety.
